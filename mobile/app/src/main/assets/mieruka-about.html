Mieruka見える化 · "making it visible"

Electricity is invisible until the bill arrives. Mieruka shows each household where its electricity goes, warns about waste before the bill, and gives STEG an anonymous overview of demand.

How it works

  1. Pair an adapter by its MIERUKA-xxxx ID or QR code.

  2. Watch each appliance's consumption as a live curve.

  3. When an appliance drifts from its usual pattern, the AI explains why and suggests a fix.

  4. Apply the tip, see consumption drop, and earn points for the kWh you avoided.

Modules

  • M1Adapter and simulator: readings, demo scenarios, offline mode
  • M2API and data: storage, pairing, alerts
  • M3AI anomalies: median rule, explanation, recommendation
  • M4User space and rewards: household screens, fair points
  • M5Back office: devices, users, usage, alerts and credits
  • M10User management: sign in with Google

Our principles

  • The prototype runs on simulated data, and says so on screen.
  • Nobody is ever cut off.
  • Rewards compare each home to itself, not to its neighbours.
  • STEG only sees regional totals, never a single household.

"We don't sell a meter; we make electricity readable."
